==============================================================================
  6. LIMITATIONS / LEAKAGE NOTES
==============================================================================
- This model uses this match's own team_xg, team_xga and pass-network
  features. It answers 'given the chances created and the way the team
  passed THIS match, how much did cohesion explain the scoreline' --
  it is NOT a pre-match forecast and should not be sold as one.
- 2015/16 league season is the large majority of rows; leave-one-season-out
  results on the small seasons (~100-128 rows each) are directional, not precise.
- Permutation importance above is computed on the same full data the final
  model was fit on (in-sample) -- it shows what the model leans on, not an
  unbiased held-out estimate. Treat magnitudes as relative, not absolute.
- Existing artifacts (scaler.pkl, gbr.pkl, ridge.pkl, graph_features.parquet)
  and api_server.py are byte-for-byte untouched by this pipeline.
